Why we started

Lots of smart students and professionals hold back. Not because they don't know enough, but because they can't say it with confidence. Voice Tech Nepal is here to close that gap.

We think speaking is a skill, not a talent. So our classes are mostly speaking time, not lectures. You practise, get feedback, and try again.

The room is safe. No shaming, no harsh judgement. You get simple frameworks for structure and storytelling, built around the situations people in Nepal actually face.

From the stage

Practise at home

Quick tips and free links to keep improving between classes.

Calm your stage nerves

Stage fear is your body trying to protect you. Slow breathing, reframing the fear, and small doses of practice all help it settle.

Coping with presentation nerves, on SkillsYouNeed

Tell a story people remember

Most good stories follow one shape: setup, conflict, change, message. Use it for speeches, interviews and even toasts.

Record yourself for two minutes a day

Pick a topic, talk for two minutes, then play it back. Note one thing to fix tomorrow. That's it.

IELTS and PTE speaking

Answer the question, add detail, then give an example. It works in every part of the speaking test.

Free IELTS lessons on IELTS Liz
Official PTE practice from Pearson

Daily English for Nepali speakers

Try shadowing: play a short clip, repeat it straight after, and match the rhythm. Five minutes a day adds up fast.

Free English resources from EF
Listen-and-repeat sounds on Pronuncian
